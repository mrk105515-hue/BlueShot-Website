1. The World Order

Earth is ruled by Four Regional Emperors and governed by One Central Supreme Judge:

  • ZIGO DEGAN (Zygo Degan): East Ruler
  • ICE KING: North Ruler
  • MARCLE: South Ruler
  • HELL: West Ruler
  • ERIC: Supreme Earth King • Central Judge above them all

3. Current World Conflict: Hell vs. Zygo

Hell and Zygo Degan were once like brothers—an unstoppable duo praised as greater than Century Legend Rezok himself.

The Catalyst of War

Zygo Degan unexpectedly murdered Hell's fiancée, Tressa Forst, and slew the father of his own wife, Koyo. With no explanation provided, Zygo's betrayal split the brotherhood, turning Hell into an avenging emperor.

To enact total revenge, Hell partnered with Devil Priest Kraven and targeted Flama Nation:

  • The 5 Billion Cash Heist: Hell made a deal with Bezrk to loot 5 billion in currency, using the heist as a tactical diversion.
  • The Real Goal: COMPLETE DEVASTATION OF FLAMA NATION.
  • The Defense: BSG, Suma, Kan, Berry, and the Three Shades are currently fighting to prevent the downfall of Flama Nation.

4. The 7,999-Year Curse God & The Devil Priest Clan

Trapped in Hell for nearly 7,999 years, the Curse God represents pure primordial ruin.

  • The Awakening Ritual (Episode 24): Devil Priest Kraven used Zalta's ancient Herobrine bloodline to unseal the Curse God.
  • Berry's Reckless Duel: Berry fought the Curse God purely for fun and is now suffering a life-and-death crisis.
  • The Sealing Key (Eysa): Kraven's sister, Eysa, holds the secret vocal mantra capable of resealing the Curse God.
