Succession & Family Bonds

After Zabong's death, Hell became Rezok's new Left-Hand Man.

As Hell and Toko grew up, they fell in love. Even though they were called family, most people in the family were not related by blood. They called each other brother, sister, uncle, or aunt because they cared about and respected one another. For example, Suma was called Hell's sister, even though they were not really brother and sister. In this family, love and friendship mattered more than blood relations.

3. Current World Conflict: Hell & Zygo

Two brothers, who were once like brothers. An unstoppable duo, together they were even greater than the Century Legend himself.

Zygo's life was calm: his wife Koyo, his adopted kids Berry and Suma. Hell and his fiancée, Tressa Forst, lived a happy life for many years. Then something terrible happened one day:

Zygo Degan killed Hell's fiancée, Tressa Forst. He even killed the father of his own wife, Koyo. Why did he do it? No answers. But those actions turned his own family against him.

Now the greatest duo fell apart and became the greatest enemies. For many years Hell has been waiting for the moment to destroy Zygo, and the time is here. He built a great plan and formed an alliance with the Devil Priest, Kraven.

4. The Curse God & The Flama Nation War

BSG's friend, Zalta, belongs to an ancient bloodline of Herobrines. Only a Devil Priest can awaken the CURSE GOD through Zalta.

And in Episode 24... Kraven did it. He awakened the Curse God, who had been trapped in Hell for nearly 7,999 years.

Then Hell targeted an entire nation: Flama Nation. He started a war. Hell made a deal with Bezrk to steal 5 billion in cash, but the heist was only a small part of the plan. Hell's real goal was simple: COMPLETE DEVASTATION OF FLAMA NATION.

Now BSG, Suma, Kan, Berry, and the Three Shades are trying to stop Hell's plan:

  • Berry's Crisis: Berry fought the Curse God just for fun and is now suffering through a life-and-death crisis. He wields extreme speed and the world's most powerful sword, Ogre.
  • Eysa: Kraven's sister and Devil Priest survivor, whose words can possess minds and who holds the mantra to seal the Curse God.
  • Kan: One-eyed warrior wielding Blue Pens (teleportation) and Red Pens (explosives).
  • Suma: Unpredictable strategist using Weak Point to cause tectonic earthquakes and underground explosions.

5. The Three Shades

Old friends of Kan and allies of his team trying to stop Hell's war:

  • Shane: The Gunman.
  • Gain: Possesses immense monster-like strength.
  • Fin: A multi-talented man with countless skills (fighting, guitar, and much more). He can kill you with almost anything.
