Danger X Zone Universe Lore

1. The World Order & Earth King Eric

Earth is divided and governed by Four Regional Emperors, all overseen by One Supreme Central Judge:

  • ZIGO DEGAN (Zygo Degan): East Ruler • Master strategist and former Right Hand of Rezok.
  • ICE KING: North Ruler • Sovereign of the frozen northern tundras.
  • MARCLE: South Ruler • Emperor of the southern continental realms.
  • HELL: West Ruler • The living anti-Rezok weapon and Left Hand of the golden era.
  • ERIC (Earth King): Supreme Central Judge • The undisputed ruler positioned above all four emperors.

3. Current World Conflict: Hell vs. Zygo

Hell and Zygo Degan were once like brothers—an unstoppable duo praised across the realm as greater even than the Century Legend himself.

Their brotherhood was shattered when Zygo Degan unexpectedly murdered Hell's fiancée, Tressa Forst, and slew the father of his own wife, Koyo. With no explanation provided, Zygo's betrayal split the brotherhood, turning Hell into an avenging emperor.

To enact total revenge, Hell formed an alliance with Devil Priest Kraven and launched the Flama Nation War. While Hell made a deal with Bezrk to loot 5 Billion in cash, the heist was merely a minor diversion; his true goal is the COMPLETE DEVASTATION OF FLAMA NATION.

4. The 7,999-Year Curse God & The Devil Priest Clan

Trapped in Hell for nearly 7,999 years, the Curse God represents pure primordial ruin.

  • Episode 24 Awakening: Devil Priest Kraven used Zalta's ancient Herobrine bloodline to rupture the seals and awaken the Curse God.
  • Berry's Crisis: Berry recklessly fought the Curse God for the thrill of the gamble, leaving him in a life-and-death crisis.
  • Eysa's Sealing Key: Kraven's sister, Eysa, commands minds with spoken words and holds the sacred mantra capable of resealing the Curse God.
