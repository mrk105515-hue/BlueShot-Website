Ziggo Degan

The East Ruler of the World Order, former Right-Hand Man of Rezok, husband to Koyo, and adoptive father of Berry & Suma, whose inexplicable execution of Tressa Forst ignited a global war.

"I stood beside Rezok as his Right Hand. But some truths are too heavy for words."

Rezok's Inner Circle & The World Order

Ziggo Degan (Zygo Degan) is one of the most powerful and enigmatic figures in the Danger X Zone universe. In the historic golden era, he served as the trusted Right-Hand Man to Century Legend Rezok within Rezok's Inner Circle ("The Six Who Ruled An Era").

Following Rezok's passing, Ziggo rose to govern the Eastern continent as the East Ruler among the 4 Earth Emperors under Supreme Judge Earth King Eric.

Family & Brotherhood with Hell

Ziggo lived a peaceful life with his wife Koyo (a core member of Rezok's inner circle) and their two adopted children, Berry and Suma.

Together with Hell, Ziggo formed an unstoppable brotherhood. As a fighting duo, their combined tactical and martial prowess surpassed even the Century Legend himself.

The Unexplained Tragedy

Without warning, this idyllic brotherhood was destroyed forever. Ziggo Degan murdered Hell's fiancée, Tressa Forst, and also killed the father of his own wife, Koyo.

Why did he do it? There were no answers given. This horrifying act turned his own family against him, split the greatest duo in history into bitter mortal enemies, and set into motion Hell's revenge campaign that now threatens Flama Nation.
