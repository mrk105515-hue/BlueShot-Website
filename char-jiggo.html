Emperor Jiggo Degan

The god-tier ruler of the Eastern Empire, legendary former left hand of an ancient deity, and the master strategist orchestrating the grand alliance against Hell.

"Jiggo Degan is the god-tier person. He is the Emperor and he has lots of hidden secrets."

Imperial Origins & The Eastern Dynasty

Emperor Jiggo Degan stands as the monumental pillar of authority, ancient wisdom, and metaphysical power in the Danger X Zone cosmos. As the sovereign ruler of the vast Eastern Empire, Jiggo governs realms fortified with celestial wards and ancestral martial academies.

Centuries before the current timeline, Jiggo served as the legendary "Left Hand" to a primordial historical deity whose name has been deliberately purged from mortal historical records. Through this service, Jiggo inherited transcendent cosmic knowledge regarding the origin of the Beyond State, the sealing of the Redago Celestial Beast, and the true existential threat posed by the Western Empire.

The Dark Dimension Passage & The Treaty with King Cobra

While mortal factions fear the demonic void known as the Dark Dimension, Emperor Jiggo Degan is one of the extraordinarily rare beings capable of opening dimensional corridors into its abyss without succumbing to madness or spiritual decay.

Recognizing that the tyrant Hell was actively assembling God-Gifted weapons to purge the mortal plane, Jiggo undertook a perilous diplomatic mission into the nether realms. There, he engaged in tense negotiations with Cobra, the Devil King, securing a non-aggression pact and dimensional transit rights to strategically outmaneuver Hell's vanguard forces in the oncoming world war.

Imperial Intellect & Chessmaster Strategy

Jiggo rarely engages in impulsive combat. With an intellect rating of 98%, he operates dozens of steps ahead of both allies and adversaries, orchestrating troop movements, celestial unsealings, and weapon acquisitions across multiple dimensions simultaneously.

Weapons, Sage Sorcery & Imperial Techniques

  • Imperial Sage Staff: An ancient artifact forged from star-core alloy that focuses cosmic energy, generates impenetrable barrier spheres, and channels elemental lightning.
  • Dark Dimension Passage: Jiggo's signature spatial manipulation technique, enabling instantaneous group teleportation and the summoning of nether-bound defense barriers.
  • Sovereign Domain Suppression: A passive imperial pressure aura that paralyzes low-tier combatants and forces intermediate warriors to their knees under gravitational weight.
  • Divination of the Stars: The ability to read temporal flow and predict enemy tactical formations hours before they materialize on the battlefield.

Family, Lineage & Imperial Court

  • Suma (Daughter): Jiggo's biological daughter and the brilliant tactical commander of the alliance. Jiggo personally trained Suma in logistics, martial analysis, and diplomatic strategy.
  • Berry Cade (Adopted Son): Taken in by Jiggo at a young age after displaying extraordinary physical endurance, Berry was gifted the legendary 'Ogre' greatsword and trained as the empire's frontline vanguard.
  • BSG: Jiggo views BSG not merely as a fugitive warrior, but as the cosmic catalyst destined to determine the survival of the universe. He provides critical guidance to help BSG tame the Flame Devil state.

Strategic Role in the World War

In Season 3, Emperor Jiggo Degan serves as the supreme commander of the united alliance. Balancing the raw, unpredictable fury of BSG and Zalta with the surgical discipline of Berry Cade and Suma, Jiggo prepares his empire for the inevitable cataclysmic clash against Emperor Hell's God-Gifted vanguard.
