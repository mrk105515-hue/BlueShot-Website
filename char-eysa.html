Eysa

The newly arrived survivor of the Devil Priest Clan and sister of Kraven, who can possess minds with her spoken words and holds the sacred mantra capable of sealing the Curse God.

"I have never known true happiness, only a terrifying past. But with this mantra, I will seal the Curse God."

Background: The Devil Priest Clan

Eysa is a newly arrived member of BSG's alliance. She has endured a terrifying childhood and has never experienced true happiness. She is the sister of Kraven (the Devil Priest who awakened the Curse God), making them the last two surviving members of the ancient Devil Priest Clan.

Unique Abilities & The Sealing Mantra

  • Mind Possession: Eysa's unique vocal enchantment allows her to temporarily possess and control the minds and bodies of others with her spoken words for a few seconds.
  • The Sacred Mantra: Eysa holds the sacred ancestral mantra of the Devil Priest Clan—the only incantation in existence capable of binding and sealing the 7,999-year-old Curse God.
