Eysa

Sister of Kraven • Devil Priest Clan Survivor • Sealer of the Curse God

"A newly arrived member of the group. She has never felt true happiness, only a terrifying past."

Origin & Background

Eysa is a newly arrived member of the group who has never felt true happiness, bearing only a terrifying past.

She is the sister of Devil Priest Kraven, making her one of the last two survivors of the Devil Priest Clan.

Abilities: Mind Control & Sealing Mantra

Her unique abilities include:

  • Mind Possession: Her unique ability allows her to possess people's minds with her words for a few seconds.
  • The Sealing Mantra: With a powerful sacred mantra, she has the power to seal the 7,999-year Curse God.
