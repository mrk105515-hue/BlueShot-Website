Biological Son of Rezok • Limitless Redago

BSG

The traumatized child forced to execute his caretaker Masa, who bore the 18-year masked robber persona to keep innocents away while carrying the limitless Redago force of Century Legend Rezok.

"For 18 years I robbed villagers so they would hate me and stay away... because those evil devils kill every single person I love."

Childhood Trauma & Masa's Sacrifice

At just 5 years old, BSG was found running in absolute terror through an unknown village. The compassionate villagers took him in, offering warmth and care. A gentle woman named Masa nursed him with medicine, gave him food, and sang him lullabies (Lori) so the trembling boy could finally find peace and sleep.

In the middle of the night, catastrophe struck. Mysterious villains engulfed the entire village in roaring flames, brutally slaughtering every resident. The mysterious leader captured Masa and dragged her before 5-year-old BSG, handing the shaking child an axe. The villains gave BSG a horrifying choice: execute Masa with the axe, and they would spare the remaining villagers.

With tears in her eyes, Masa smiled gently and whispered: "Do it... BSG." Heartbroken and shaking, BSG swung the axe and severed Masa's head. The mysterious leader laughed mockingly—"That's my boy..."—and the villains proceeded to mercilessly butcher every single villager anyway.

The 18-Year Mask & The Bloodline of Rezok

Traumatized and hollow, BSG fled from village to village, but catastrophic destruction followed him everywhere. Oro, Masa, and over 30 loved ones were slaughtered, and 10 entire villages were burned to the ground by the relentless forces hunting him.

Why was he hunted? Because BSG is the biological son of Century Legend Rezok and his wife Oma. From his father's bloodline, BSG carries the limitless force of Redago. Rezok was killed in war, and the same evil devils murdered BSG's mother.

To prevent anyone else from dying because of him, BSG hardened his mentality for 18 years. He wore a mask and began robbing villagers so that everyone would despise and avoid him. By forcing people to hate him, he ensured no one would ever form an emotional bond with him and become a target of the devils chasing his bloodline.

Mentors, Battles & The Vanguard

While traveling through a dense forest, BSG encountered an innocent-looking physician named Doctor Black Dagger (Scinto). Scinto offered guidance and became BSG's mentor, pretending to be a weak human while secretly observing BSG's awakening Redago power to steal it.

Along his journey, BSG clashed with Zalta, an enemy manipulated by Emperor Hell. After BSG defeated Zalta in combat, Zalta discovered Hell's deception and became BSG's loyal teammate. BSG later met his true mentors, Koyo and Ziggo Degan, uniting a vanguard consisting of Suma, Berry, Kan, Scinto, Zalta, and allies the Three Shades.

BSG's Ultimate Goal

From the very start, BSG has held one singular mission: uncover the true mastermind behind the slaughter of all his loved ones, exact his revenge, and explore the vast universe far beyond.

Combat Style & Core Powers

  • Limitless Redago Bloodline: Inherited directly from Century Legend Rezok; an inexhaustible, primordial well of spiritual energy.
  • Flame Devil Form: The first awakened combat transformation of Redago, unleashing blazing kinetic aura and extreme physical power.
  • Herobrine Sensory Eyes: Glowing white irises capable of perceiving hidden intents, aura fluctuations, and spatial anomalies.
  • Iron Resolve: 18 years of solitary survival and immense psychological suffering have forged BSG into an unbreakable warrior.
