Official Character Dossier

BSG

Son of Century Legend Rezok • Carrier of Limitless Redago

"Wherever I go, only destruction follows. They killed all the loved ones of BSG... So I rob villagers and make them hate me to keep them safe."

The 5-Year-Old Boy & The Tragedy of Masa

A 5-year-old boy, scared, running through a village. He meets a lot of villagers. The villagers ask: "What happened? Boy, are you okay?" Showing care and empathy, the boy calms down. He fell down and became unconscious…

When he wakes up, he sees he is being cared for. A woman, Masa, is giving him medicine. At first he was confused, but then they give him food and love. The boy slowly calms down; for a few days he found peace. He was afraid of something and couldn't sleep because of that, but a woman, Masa, calms him down and tells him a lori (lullaby). The boy's eyes get heavy and he fell asleep.

In the midnight, his eyes wake up; he was hearing some noise. He wakes up…… and suddenly he sees the whole village is on fire, people brutally getting killed. The boy was shocked—they were the same people he was running from… he got completely stunned.

Now the Mysterious Man is laughing: "Uhhhh, so good morning... oh no, bad morning, boy…."
Boy: "Leave them, you bastard…!"

Those people bring Masa, the same woman who had been taking care of BSG all those days. Those mysterious people force BSG and give him a choice: kill this woman and we will leave…
He was crying—imagine his situation, his hands were shaking…
Masa, smiling: "Do it… BSG."

BSG was the name of this boy, his heart broken. An axe was in his hand and he was forced to kill her… The mysterious people, one by one, start killing everyone. BSG's hands trembling, he was in shock…
BSG: "Ahhhh!!!!! Please… no!"
And he cut off the head of Masa…

BSG heartbroken… He falls to the ground… The mysterious people fall silent, stopping the violence…
BSG crying loudly…
Mysterious Man: "That's my boy…."
BSG looks up… and he sees that the mysterious people still brutally killed all the villagers.

BSG got traumatized. He falls to the ground and becomes unconscious. When he wakes up in the morning, those evil people were not there… all left are the corpses of the people he knew…

The Cursed Boy & The 18-Year Mask

But it's not one time… BSG runs through village to village, and the same tragedy follows him. He became like a cursed boy—wherever he goes, only destruction follows… They killed all the loved ones of BSG: Oro, Masa, and 30 others, and 10 villages destroyed. BSG runs and the devils behind him follow…

Why were they chasing him? Because BSG is the son of Century Legend Rezok and he carries the limitless force Redago from his bloodline. Rezok got killed in war, and these devils killed BSG's mother as well. BSG runs and runs and runs, but can't escape them…

After 18 years, BSG's mentality became so harsh. Now he robs villagers so people will hate him. He doesn't love anyone, doesn't befriend anyone, because those evil people kill the people he loves.

Meeting Mentors & Building the Team

One day, BSG was going through the forest. He meets a doctor, Doctor Black Dagger (Scinto). That's how his journey begins; he becomes his mentor. Later he meets so many people: Zalta was once his enemy for a long time, and after BSG defeats him, Zalta becomes his teammate.

He later meets his true mentors, Koyo and Zigo Degan, and his whole team: Suma, Berry, Kan, Scinto, Zalta, and now allies like the Three Shades.

BSG's Ultimate Goal

He has one goal from the very start: to find out the mastermind behind who killed all his loved ones. It's the journey of his revenge, then to explore the world far and beyond.
