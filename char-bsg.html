Official Character Dossier

BSG

Son of Century Legend Rezok • Carrier of Limitless Redago

"Wherever I go, only destruction follows. They killed all the people I loved... So I wore a mask and made everyone hate me to keep them safe."

Childhood & The Tragedy of Masa

At 5 years old, BSG was seen running through a village, terrified and scared. He met a lot of villagers who asked him with care and empathy, "What happened? Boy, are you okay?" The boy calmed down, fell down, and became unconscious.

When he woke up, he saw he was being cared for. A woman named Masa was giving him medicine. At first he was confused, but then they gave him food and love. For a few days he found peace, though he remained afraid of something and could not sleep. Masa calmed him down and sang him a lullaby (Lori) until his eyes grew heavy and he fell asleep.

In the middle of the night, he woke up to loud noises and suddenly saw the whole village in flames, with people being brutally killed. The boy was shocked—they were the same people he was running from. A mysterious man laughed: "Uhhhh, so good morning... oh no, bad morning, boy..."

BSG shouted: "Leave them, you bastard!"

Those people brought Masa, the same woman who had been taking care of BSG. The mysterious people forced BSG and gave him a choice: kill this woman, and they would leave the rest. BSG was crying, his hands shaking. Masa smiled at him and said: "Do it... BSG."

An axe was placed in 5-year-old BSG's hands, and the villains began killing everyone one by one. Trembling in shock, BSG cried out, "Ahhhh!!!!! Please... no," and cut off the head of Masa.

Heartbroken, BSG fell to the ground crying loudly as the mysterious people fell silent. The mysterious man said, "That's my boy..." BSG looked up and saw that the mysterious people had still brutally slaughtered all the villagers. Traumatized, BSG fell to the ground unconscious. When he woke up in the morning, the evil people were gone; all that was left were the corpses of the people he knew.

The 18-Year Mask & The Hunt for His Bloodline

This was not a one-time tragedy. BSG ran from village to village, but the same tragedy followed him wherever he went. He became known as a cursed boy—wherever he went, only destruction followed. The forces hunting him killed all of BSG's loved ones: Oro, Masa, and 30 others, destroying 10 entire villages.

Why were they chasing him? Because BSG is the biological son of Century Legend Rezok, and he carries the limitless force of Redago in his bloodline. Rezok was killed in war, and these devils killed BSG's mother as well. BSG ran and ran, but could not escape them.

After 18 years, BSG's mentality became very harsh. He began robbing villagers so people would hate him. He made a vow not to love anyone or befriend anyone, because those evil forces always killed the people he cared about.

Meeting Mentors & Building the Team

One day while traveling through the forest, BSG met a doctor named Black Dagger (Scinto). That was how his journey began; Scinto became his mentor, teaching BSG while secretly observing his limitless Redago power to steal it.

Later, BSG met many comrades. Zalta was his enemy for a long time, but after BSG defeated him, Zalta discovered he had been manipulated by Emperor Hell and became BSG's teammate. BSG later met his true mentors, Koyo and Zigo Degan, and united with the whole team: Suma, Berry, Kan, Scinto, Zalta, and allies the Three Shades.

BSG's Ultimate Goal

From the very start, BSG has had one goal: to find out the mastermind behind who killed all his loved ones, complete his revenge, and explore the world far and beyond.
