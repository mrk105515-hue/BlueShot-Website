Official Character Dossier

Zalta

Last Survivor of the Herobrine Clan • Son of Zabong

"I saw my father's dead body and was consumed by rage. Hell manipulated me for 25 years until I fought BSG and discovered the truth."

Clan Wipeout & Zabong's Death

Zalta is the last survivor of the Herobrine Clan. His entire clan was wiped out by Sage Monk and Century Legend Rezok.

Twenty-five years ago, Rezok also killed Zalta's father, Zabong (Rezok's former Left-Hand Man). Zalta was only a child at the time and never knew about his father's dirty deeds. When he saw his father's dead body before his eyes, his heart shattered.

Consumed by rage and driven by a thirst for revenge, Zalta became a criminal. He was manipulated by a mysterious man who was later revealed to be Emperor Hell.

Westo City Battle & Ally to BSG

Twenty-five years later, Zalta's fate took a turn when he attacked Westo City and met BSG for the very first time. After battling and being defeated by BSG, Zalta realized something much bigger: he discovered that he had been manipulated by Hell all along.

Because of this revelation, Zalta joined BSG's side, and they fought together as teammates for a long time.

Episode 24: Awakening of the Curse God

BSG's friend Zalta belongs to the ancient bloodline of Herobrines. Only a Devil Priest can awaken the Curse God through Zalta.

In Episode 24, Kraven (the last Devil Priest) did it: he used Zalta's ancient Herobrine bloodline to awaken the Curse God, who had been trapped in Hell for nearly 7,999 years, changing everything.
