Curse God Zalta

The last surviving descendant of an ancient cursed dynasty, possessing the terrifying spectral power of the Dark Bull and achieving a 50x power surge post-escape.

"Zalta has awakened Beast Mode 2nd Form. And has almost killed Lord Cage."

Character Biography & Lineage

Curse God Zalta is one of the most formidable, volatile fighters in the Danger X Zone storyline. As the sole inheritor of a catastrophic bloodline curse that decimated his ancestral clan, Zalta was viewed by the ruling factions as a living weapon of mass destruction. Feared by both the Eastern and Western Empires, he was incarcerated in the deepest subterranean sectors of the Infinity Prison alongside BSG.

During their prolonged imprisonment, Zalta forged an unbreakable brotherhood with BSG. When the prison defenses faltered, Zalta broke his spectral shackles, initiating a chain of events that permanently disrupted the multiversal power hierarchy.

The 50x Power Explosion & Beast Mode 2nd Form

Following his release into the mortal realm, Zalta experienced an unprecedented biological and spiritual adaptation. Within just one single week of breathing free air, his internal energy channels evolved rapidly, resulting in a staggering 50x multiplication of his base combat power.

This exponential power leap culminated in the awakening of his Beast Mode 2nd Form (Dark Bull). In this state, towering spectral horns materialize from his cranium, his musculature expands with dense shadow essence, and his eyes radiate a piercing demonic crimson glow. With this raw physical and spectral enhancement, Zalta engaged the feared imperial enforcer Lord Cage, delivering a devastating near-fatal beating that sent shockwaves through the syndicate ranks.

The Cursed Bloodline Paradox

Unlike celestial powers which rely on inner tranquility and alignment, Zalta's curse converts biological trauma and pain directly into kinetic destructive force. The closer Zalta is pushed toward mortal collapse, the denser and more violent his Dark Bull spectral aura becomes.

Weapons, Techniques & Combat Arsenal

  • Spectral Shadow Horns & Claws: Manifestations of concentrated dark matter capable of tearing through enchanted armor and dimensional barriers.
  • Dark Bull Charge (Special Technique): Zalta accelerates to hypersonic speeds, cloaked in the spectral silhouette of an apocalyptic raging bull, obliterating all physical obstacles in his trajectory.
  • Curse Regeneration: Rapid cellular recovery fueled by his dark bloodline, allowing him to heal severe puncture wounds and broken bones mid-combat.
  • Kinetic Shockwave Stomp: Channels curse energy into the ground to produce localized earthquakes, disrupting enemy formations and stunning vanguard units.

Key Battles & Historical Encounters

  • The Infinity Prison Breach: Partnered with BSG to overpower the Elite Titan Wardens and shatter the anti-magic dampening crystals.
  • The Battle Against Lord Cage: Zalta's signature Season 3 confrontation where he unleashed Beast Mode 2nd Form, brutally overwhelming one of the world's most feared combatants.
  • Defense of the Valley: Held off waves of DXZ cybernetic assassins, buying crucial time for Suma and Berry Cade to establish their tactical perimeter.

Lore Connections & Relationships

  • BSG: The only person Zalta considers his true equal and spiritual kin. Their combined might represents the greatest dual threat to Emperor Hell's reign.
  • Lord Cage: Zalta's bitter nemesis whom he brought to the brink of death, creating a lingering vendetta that fuels ongoing storylines.
  • Berry Cade: Though their combat styles differ—Berry favoring disciplined blade techniques and Zalta utilizing primal beast fury—the two share mutual respect on the battlefield.
