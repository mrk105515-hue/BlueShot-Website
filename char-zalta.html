Curse God Zalta

The last survivor of the Herobrine Clan whose father Zabong was slain by Rezok, who was manipulated by Hell until redeeming himself alongside BSG, and whose body was awakened as the Curse God in Episode 24.

"Hell told me my father's death was unjust... but when I fought BSG, I saw the truth. I am no longer Hell's puppet."

Extinction of the Herobrine Clan & Zabong's Death

Zalta is the sole remaining survivor of the ancient and feared Herobrine Clan. His entire clan was wiped out in a historic war by Sage Monk and Century Legend Rezok.

Twenty-five years ago, Rezok executed Zalta's father, Zabong (Rezok's former Left-Hand Man). Young Zalta was merely a child and never knew about his father's corrupt deeds. Witnessing his father's dead body shattered Zalta's heart, filling him with an insatiable thirst for vengeance.

Hell's Deception & The Westo City Encounter

Consumed by grief and rage, Zalta was manipulated by Emperor Hell, who used Zalta as an assassin for 25 years. Hell directed Zalta to assault Westo City, where Zalta clashed with BSG for the first time.

Following a fierce battle where BSG defeated him, Zalta discovered that Hell had been orchestrating his entire life as a disposable tool. Overcome by remorse, Zalta joined BSG's side and became one of his most loyal combat teammates.

Episode 24: Awakening of the Curse God

Because Zalta carries the ancient royal bloodline of the Herobrine Clan, his mortal body serves as the cosmic key to the primordial seals. Only a Devil Priest possesses the dark incantations to unlock this power.

In Episode 24, the last Devil Priest, Kraven, performed the ritual and awakened the Curse God—an apocalyptic entity that had been sealed in Hell for nearly 7,999 years.

Current Crisis

With the 7,999-year Curse God unsealed inside Zalta, BSG, Suma, Berry, Kan, and the Three Shades are in a desperate race against time to stop Hell's destruction of Flama Nation and seal the Curse God using Eysa's ancient mantra.
