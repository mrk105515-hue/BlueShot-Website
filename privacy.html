Privacy Policy

Last Updated: November 3, 2026

Welcome to The BlueShot ("we", "our", or "us"). We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting the personal data of our users. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and share information when you visit or interact with our website at https://blueshot.online.

1. Information We Collect

When you interact with our website, join our community, or place an order, we collect the following types of information:

  • Personal Details: Name, Gamer Tag, email address, shipping address, and billing address.
  • Order & Supporter Details: Items purchased, rank contributions, faction affiliation, and payment transaction reference IDs.
  • Device & Logging Info: IP address, browser type, operating system, pages visited, and cookies to ensure optimal functionality.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information for the following business purposes:

  • To operate and maintain the website, interactive games, and community leaderboards.
  • To process payments and fulfill merchandise orders securely.
  • To email you receipts, order updates, account details, and support notifications.
  • To protect our website against fraudulent attempts, unauthorized usage, and security vulnerabilities.
  • To analyze user engagement and improve the Danger X Zone experience.

3. Google AdSense & Third-Party Advertising Cookies

We partner with Google and other third-party advertising networks to serve advertisements when you visit our website. Please review the following important information regarding third-party advertising:

  • Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on a user's prior visits to this website or other websites on the Internet.
  • Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to our users based on their visits to our site and/or other sites across the World Wide Web.
  • You may opt out of personalized advertising at any time by visiting Google Ads Settings.
  • Alternatively, you can opt out of a third-party vendor's use of cookies for interest-based advertising by visiting www.aboutads.info or the Digital Advertising Alliance.

4. Cookies & Web Beacons

Our website uses cookies (small text files stored on your device) to recognize your browser, maintain session state, remember cart items, and provide aggregated analytics. You can choose to disable cookies through your individual browser settings; however, doing so may affect certain interactive features of our website.

5. Payment Processing & Data Security

All payments are securely processed by Razorpay. We do not store or capture your credit/debit card numbers, UPI PINs, or banking passwords on our servers. Razorpay handles your financial credentials in an encrypted secure framework compliant with global PCI-DSS security standards.

6. Sharing Your Information

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information. We only share transaction details with our payment partner (Razorpay) and fulfillment agents solely to process your orders and ship your packages.

7. Your Rights & Data Retention

If you reside in India or are protected under GDPR, CCPA, or similar regional data protection frameworks, you have the right to request access to the personal data we hold about you, request corrections, or request deletion of your contact data. Feel free to contact us via the email below to execute these rights.

8. Contact Us

For any questions regarding this Privacy Policy, your personal data, or our advertising practices, please contact us at blueshotconnectx@gmail.com.
