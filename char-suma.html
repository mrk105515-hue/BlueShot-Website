Suma (Tactical Mind)

The brilliant daughter of Emperor Jiggo Degan and the supreme tactical brain of the alliance, orchestrating multi-front military operations with an unmatched 99% intellect rating.

"Suma is older than Berry Cade. She is the daughter of Jiggo Degan."

Imperial Lineage & Intellectual Supremacy

Within the complex socio-political landscape of Danger X Zone, Suma represents the pinnacle of intellectual discipline, strategic foresight, and royal composure. As the biological daughter of Emperor Jiggo Degan, Suma was raised inside the grand archives of the Eastern Empire, studying ancient military treaties, energy warfare, and the metaphysical properties of dimensional gateways from early childhood.

Renowned across all four quadrants of the continent for combining radiant elegance with a razor-sharp intellect (rated at a staggering 99%), Suma serves as the de facto operational field commander for the united faction alliance.

Command Philosophy & Battlefield Orchestration

Advertisement

While frontline heavyweights like BSG and Berry Cade unleash overwhelming physical firepower, it is Suma's calculated logistics and split-second tactical calls that ensure those attacks land where they inflict the maximum strategic damage.

Suma processes battlefield variables simultaneously: calculating enemy reinforcements, energy depletion curves, dimensional wind resistance, and escape trajectories in real-time. Under her calm direction, even overwhelming odds—such as imperial assassin sieges—are systematically disassembled into manageable, isolated skirmishes.

The Sibling Dynamic with Berry Cade

Although Berry Cade is Emperor Jiggo Degan's adopted son and technically Suma's adoptive younger brother, their relationship on the battlefield is one of complete harmony. Suma identifies the weak point in the enemy line, and Berry serves as her unstoppable battering ram with the Ogre greatsword.

Weapons, Techniques & Combat Arsenal

  • Chao Tactical Blades: Twin ultra-light enchanted daggers crafted from celestial alloy, allowing Suma to parry heavier weapons and strike vulnerable joint seams with blinding speed.
  • Absolute Command (Ultimate Move): A tactical synchronization technique where Suma broadcasts strategic telepathic cues to all allied combatants, synchronizing their simultaneous strikes to shatter enemy defensive formations.
  • Vanguard Strike: An agile flanking maneuver where Suma exploits enemy blind spots created by Berry Cade's heavy swings to deliver precise disabling cuts.
  • Holographic Tactical Grid: A portable wrist-mounted imperial interface projecting 3D terrain maps, troop health telemetry, and dimensional rift markers.

Key Relationships & Family Bonds

  • Emperor Jiggo Degan (Father): Suma deeply respects her father's ancient wisdom and shares his heavy burden of preparing the world for the clash against Emperor Hell.
  • Berry Cade (Brother): Suma often acts as the guiding anchor for Berry's aggressive vanguard instincts, ensuring his bravery is never exploited by enemy traps.
  • Eysa: Close confidante and fellow mystic strategist. Together, they form the intellectual and spiritual nucleus of the resistance.
  • Black Dagger: Suma was among the earliest to detect anomalies in Scinto's combat analytics, leading to the eventual unmasking of his secret villainous identity.
