Suma

The unpredictable genius strategist whose greatest weapon is her intellect, able to touch tectonic weak points beneath the earth to trigger massive earthquakes and subterranean explosions.

"Every action has a purpose. Touch the weak point beneath the ground, and the entire continent shakes."

Persona & Intellect

Suma is an utterly unpredictable woman. She never makes a casual move—every single action, word, and gesture has a calculated purpose behind it. Her greatest, most formidable weapon in battle is her Brain.

Adopted by Ziggo Degan and Koyo alongside Berry, Suma was raised in the extended Rezok family where she was affectionately regarded as Hell's sister.

Unique Ability: Weak Point

Suma possesses a fearsome, unique martial talent known as Weak Point:

  • Tectonic Analysis: Suma can visually scan and calculate structural stress points hidden deep beneath the surface of the earth.
  • Seismic Detonation: By touching these subterranean weak points, she can shake tectonic plates, generate catastrophic earthquakes, and trigger massive underground shockwaves and explosions.
