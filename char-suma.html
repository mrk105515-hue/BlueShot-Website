Official Character Dossier

Suma

The Unpredictable Mastermind • Weak Point Specialist

"An unpredictable woman. Yup, that's it. She does everything for a reason. Every action has a purpose. Why does she do it? Try to figure it out, kid."

Overview & Intellect

Suma is an unpredictable woman. She does everything for a reason, and every action has a purpose. Her greatest weapon is her brain.

She is the adopted daughter of Zigo Degan and his wife Koyo, raised alongside her adopted brother Berry. Growing up in Rezok's family circle, she was considered Hell's sister.

Unique Ability: Weak Point

Suma possesses a unique ability called Weak Point.

In many episodes, she uses it on the ground itself: by touching weak points beneath the surface, she can shake tectonic plates, create earthquakes, and trigger massive underground explosions.

Now, Suma is fighting alongside BSG, Kan, Berry, and the Three Shades to stop Emperor Hell's plan.
