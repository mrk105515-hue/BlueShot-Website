Hell (The Fate Author)

The god-like tyrant who commands the Western Empire, wielding the omnipotent God-Gifted Fate-Eraser Blade to write the life and death of all mortal beings.

"Hell is the most powerful being alive, for now. Because he can kill anyone because he has a God Gifted weapon."

The Supreme Sovereign of the West

Within the overarching narrative of Danger X Zone, Hell is the absolute pinnacle of dread, tyrannical authority, and metaphysical dominance. As the undisputed Emperor of the Western Realms, Hell commands legions of mechanized cybernetic legions, corrupt dark sages, and high-tier assassins.

Unlike mortal conquerors driven by terrestrial wealth, Hell views existence as a cosmic parchment where only his will dictates reality. He operates as the self-proclaimed "Fate Author", utilizing primordial artifacts to rewrite destiny, eradicate entire bloodlines, and sever mortal souls from the cosmic cycle.

The God-Gifted Fate-Eraser Blade

Hell's absolute supremacy stems from his possession of the legendary God-Gifted Weapon. Forged outside the boundaries of time and space, this blade bypasses physical armor, regenerative factors, and energy barriers entirely:

  • Concept of Absolute Death: Any wound inflicted by the God-Gifted Blade cannot be healed by normal or cursed regeneration, making every strike permanently fatal if aimed at vital meridians.
  • World Authoring (Special Ability): Hell can manipulate localized causal reality, imposing conditions upon his opponents' movements, draining their energy, or altering terrain to ensure unavoidable fatal strikes.
  • Crimson Void Aura: Emits an oppressive cosmic gravity field rated at 100% Destructive Force, disintegrating standard steel and shattering obsidian shields upon mere proximity.
The Obsession with Redago

Despite his supreme authority, Hell knows that the unsealed 100% Redago Celestial Beast is the only cosmic entity capable of transcending his God-Gifted blade. His overarching campaign in Season 3 is aimed at capturing BSG to harvest the Redago essence and achieve eternal, unchallengeable omnipotence across all dimensions.

The Western Empire & Syndicate Alliances

Hell orchestrates the grand world war through both direct imperial edicts and covert underworld alliances:

  • The DXZ Evil Syndicate: Hell finances and equips Black Dagger's genetic research laboratories, trading high-tech weaponry in exchange for biological experimental data on Redago hosts.
  • The Imperial Inquisitors: High-ranking warlords like Lord Cage who enforce Hell's decrees across conquered provinces with brutal efficiency.
  • The Siege of the East: Hell's primary military objective—the total invasion and destruction of Emperor Jiggo Degan's sovereign territory to seize ancient celestial seals.

Ideological Clashes & Nemeses

  • BSG: Hell views BSG as his ultimate prize. Their impending confrontation is the core axis around which the entire fate of the universe revolves.
  • Emperor Jiggo Degan: Hell's political and philosophical antithesis. While Jiggo seeks balance, cultivation, and the preservation of free will, Hell demands absolute obedience and subjugation under his God-Gifted rule.
  • Curse God Zalta: Hell recognizes Zalta's 50x power surge as an unexpected wildcard that threatens his vanguard generals.
