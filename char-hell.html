Emperor Hell

Engineered 40 years ago from Rezok's blood and Devil King Cobra, adopted into Rezok's family, and now locked in a devastating war against Zygo Degan after the murder of his fiancée Tressa Forst.

"We were unstoppable brothers... until Zygo killed Tressa. Now Flama Nation will burn to ashes."

Origin: The 40-Year-Old Experiment

Forty years ago, Black Dagger Scinto sought to build the ultimate weapon capable of destroying Century Legend Rezok. Realizing that only Rezok could defeat Rezok, Scinto combined Rezok's stolen blood with the power of Devil King Cobra from another dimension. From the sole surviving surrogate mother, Hell was born.

For 14 years, Scinto tried to awaken Hell's monstrous power. When Hell failed to exhibit the ruthless destruction Scinto expected, Scinto discarded him onto the streets as a homeless orphan.

The Rezok Family & Toko's Love

While wandering the streets, homeless Hell met Rezok himself. Instead of becoming Rezok's enemy, Hell became his student and was formally adopted into the Rezok family alongside biological son BSG and adopted daughter Toko.

In Rezok's household, love and loyalty transcended bloodlines. Suma was regarded as Hell's sister. As Hell and Toko grew up together, they fell deeply in love. Following the death of Zabong, Hell was elevated to become Rezok's new Left-Hand Man in the legendary Inner Circle ("The Six Who Ruled An Era").

The Unforgiven Feud with Zygo Degan

Hell and Ziggo Degan (Zygo Degan) were once like brothers—an unstoppable duo celebrated across the realm, greater even than the Century Legend himself. Hell lived happily with his beloved fiancée, Tressa Forst, while Zygo lived with his wife Koyo and adopted kids Berry and Suma.

Then, an unthinkable tragedy shattered their world: Zygo Degan killed Hell's fiancée, Tressa Forst, and also murdered the father of his own wife, Koyo. The reasons behind Zygo's betrayal remain completely unknown.

The Flama Nation War & Curse God Awakening

Fueled by years of burning hatred, Hell orchestrated a grand master plan to annihilate Zygo. Hell formed an alliance with Devil Priest Kraven. Knowing that Zalta belongs to the ancient Herobrine bloodline, Kraven successfully awakened the Curse God (trapped in Hell for 7,999 years) during Episode 24.

Hell launched an all-out assault against Flama Nation. While he made a pact with Bezrk to steal 5 Billion in cash, the heist was merely a distraction for his true objective: THE COMPLETE DEVASTATION OF FLAMA NATION.

World Order Title

Hell rules as the West Ruler among the 4 Earth Emperors under Supreme Judge Earth King Eric.
