Official Character Dossier

Emperor Hell

West Ruler • Living Anti-Rezok Weapon • Former Left-Hand Man

"The boy created to kill Rezok became his student. But when Zygo murdered my fiancée Tressa, brotherhood became war."

Origin: The Creation of Hell

Forty years ago, one name was feared by every criminal empire on Earth: Rezok. No weapon could stop him, and no army could defeat him. One man hated that reality more than anyone: Black Dagger Scinto.

For years, Scinto studied Rezok from the shadows—every battle, every victory, every detail. Again and again he searched for a way to defeat him, and failed. Until he discovered a terrifying truth: "The only man capable of defeating Rezok... is Rezok himself."

If no weapon or army could destroy Rezok, Black Dagger decided to create another Rezok—a perfect successor and weapon. First, he obtained Rezok's blood. Then he made a dangerous deal with Devil King Cobra, a powerful being from another dimension. Many women died during the experiments; only one survived. And from that impossible union, a child was born: Hell.

Adoption into the Rezok Family

For fourteen years, Black Dagger tried to awaken Hell's power, but Hell never became the monster he expected. Furious, Black Dagger abandoned him on the streets as a homeless child—alone, forgotten, and broken.

Until fate played its cruelest joke: the boy created to kill Rezok met Rezok himself. Instead of becoming his enemy, Hell became Rezok's student and part of the Rezok family.

After Zabong's death 25 years ago, Hell became Rezok's new Left-Hand Man. As Hell and Toko grew up, they fell in love. In this household, love and friendship mattered more than blood relations; Suma was considered Hell's sister.

The Betrayal & The Flama Nation War

Hell and Zygo Degan were once like brothers—an unstoppable duo greater than the Century Legend himself. Zygo lived calmly with his wife Koyo and adopted children Berry and Suma, while Hell lived happily with his fiancée, Tressa Forst.

Then something terrible happened: Zygo Degan killed Hell's fiancée, Tressa Forst, and also killed the father of his own wife, Koyo, with no answers given.

The greatest duo fell apart and became mortal enemies. Hell spent years building a great plan to destroy Zygo. He formed an alliance with Devil Priest Kraven, who in Episode 24 awakened the 7,999-year Curse God using Zalta's Herobrine bloodline.

Hell targeted Flama Nation and started a war. Although he made a deal with Bezrk to steal 5 billion in cash, the heist was only a small part of his plan. Hell's true goal is the COMPLETE DEVASTATION OF FLAMA NATION. Now, Hell rules as the West Ruler among the 4 Emperors.
