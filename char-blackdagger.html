Official Character Dossier

Black Dagger (Doctor Scinto)

The 150-Year Mastermind • Creator of Hell

"He pretends to be nothing more than a weak human doctor, but beneath the surface lies a dark truth: he has lived for over 150 years to obtain the limitless Redago."

The False Physician in Episode 1

In Episode 1, BSG meets an apparently innocent scientist and doctor named Black Dagger (Scinto) in the forest. The two become friends and begin their journey together.

Scinto acts as BSG's mentor, teaching him while secretly observing his power, Redago, pretending to be nothing more than a weak human. But beneath the surface lies a dark truth: he has lived for over 150 years. His true goal is to obtain the limitless power of Redago from BSG.

Forty Years of Obsession & The Creation of Hell

Forty years ago, when Century Legend Rezok was feared by every criminal empire on Earth, Black Dagger Scinto hated that reality more than anyone. No weapon could stop Rezok, and no army could defeat him.

Scinto studied Rezok from the shadows—every battle, every victory, every detail—searching for a way to defeat him. After failing repeatedly, he concluded: "The only man capable of defeating Rezok... is Rezok himself."

Black Dagger decided to create another Rezok. He obtained Rezok's blood and struck a dangerous deal with Devil King Cobra from another dimension. Many women died during his experiments; only one survived, giving birth to Hell.

For fourteen years, Scinto tried to awaken Hell's power to make him a monster that could slay Rezok. When Hell failed to become a monster, a furious Black Dagger abandoned the 14-year-old child on the streets.
