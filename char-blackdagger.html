Black Dagger (Scinto)

The insidious supreme leader of the DXZ Evil Syndicate who operates in plain sight as the charming swordsman Scinto, conducting illicit genetic experiments on cosmic entities.

"Black Dagger conducts experiments on humans, demons, and devils to create brainwashed soldiers. Lightning is just a simple illusion."
Crucial Lore Revelation: Scinto and Black Dagger are the exact same individual. Scinto is merely an engineered persona used to infiltrate the Eastern Alliance and monitor BSG's awakening from within.

The Mastermind Behind the Shadows

In the intricate web of deceit that defines Danger X Zone, Black Dagger is the chief architect of terror. Operating beneath the surface of the grand imperial conflict, he founded the subterranean DXZ Evil Syndicate—a ruthless black-market conglomerate specializing in biological augmentation, soul extraction, and prohibited celestial cloning.

To monitor the awakening of the Redago beast within BSG without arousing suspicion, Black Dagger constructed the persona of Scinto: a cheerful, skilled mercenary swordsman who joined the resistance vanguard, feigning loyalty while transmitting strategic military intel to Hell's inquisitors.

Genetic Experimentation & The Brainwashed Clone Army

Black Dagger's true objective transcends ordinary imperial conquest. Operating deep within subterranean laboratories, he conducts ungodly surgical procedures on humans, devils, and fallen warriors:

  • Soul Extraction: Stripping the lifeforce from captured warriors to power experimental cybernetic frames.
  • Neural Enslavement: Injecting dark matter neuro-implants that transform proud martial artists into obedient, pain-immune shock troopers.
  • The Redago Gene Harvesting: Black Dagger's ultimate obsession—attempting to isolate and replicate BSG's celestial DNA to engineer an army of artificial Flame Devils.
Lightning as an Illusion

While opponents believe Black Dagger simply wields elemental lightning, his true combat discipline is far more sinister. The brilliant electrical arcs are merely high-frequency kinetic illusions used to mask his actual micro-cybernetic daggers and spatial displacement traps.

Weapons, Techniques & Combat Arsenal

  • Cybernetic Dagger & Lightning Blade: A deadly twin-weapon configuration featuring a high-frequency vibrating dagger paired with a sword that discharges blinding electrical plasma arcs.
  • Ragnarok Bolt (Ultimate Move): Black Dagger converges artificial lightning and dark soul energy into a singular, world-rending thunderbolt that obliterates defensive barriers.
  • Summon Cybernetic Clones: Deploys holographic decoys and brainwashed clone duplicates to overwhelm opponents through numerical saturation and confusion.
  • Neural Disruptor Pulse: Emits an electromagnetic frequency that scrambles communication devices and temporarily stuns enemy neural reflexes.

Key Deceptions & Vendettas

  • BSG: Black Dagger manipulated BSG's imprisonment from behind the scenes, viewing BSG as his ultimate genetic harvest subject.
  • Suma: Black Dagger's greatest intellectual rival. Suma's forensic military analysis continuously uncovers his syndicate supply lines, forcing him to adapt his deceptive tactics.
  • Emperor Hell: A transactional alliance; Hell supplies Black Dagger with raw materials and political immunity, while Black Dagger provides Hell with advanced cybernetic siege weaponry.
