Black Dagger (Scinto)

The insidious 150-year-old manipulator who poses as an innocent doctor to siphon BSG's limitless Redago, and who created Emperor Hell 40 years ago from Rezok's blood and Devil King Cobra.

"The only man capable of defeating Rezok... is Rezok himself."
Crucial Canon Revelation: Scinto and Black Dagger are the exact same individual. Black Dagger is an evil mastermind who has lived for over 150 years, posing as a frail scientist/doctor to steal BSG's limitless Redago.

The False Doctor of Episode 1

In Episode 1, BSG crosses paths with an apparently gentle and harmless scientist named Doctor Black Dagger (Scinto). The two become companions and begin traveling together. Scinto acts as BSG's teacher and mentor, pretending to be nothing more than a frail, ordinary human.

Beneath this benign disguise lies a sinister reality: Scinto is an evil mastermind who has survived for over 150 years. His sole motivation for mentoring BSG is to observe, nurture, and ultimately steal the limitless power of Redago from BSG's bloodline.

40 Years Ago: The Creation of Hell

Forty years ago, one name struck absolute terror into every criminal syndicate and warrior empire on Earth: Rezok. No weapon could harm him, and no army could defeat him.

Black Dagger Scinto despised Rezok's supremacy more than anyone. For years, Scinto watched Rezok from the shadows—analyzing every battle, every victory, and every technique. Every attempt Scinto made to destroy Rezok ended in failure.

Finally, Scinto arrived at a terrifying realization: "The only man capable of defeating Rezok... is Rezok himself."

Scinto obtained a sample of Rezok's blood and struck a perilous pact with Devil King Cobra, a monstrous sovereign from another dimension. Scinto conducted brutal bio-spiritual experiments on numerous women. Many perished, but one woman survived. From that unholy fusion, the living anti-Rezok weapon was born: Hell.

Abandoning 14-Year-Old Hell

For 14 years, Scinto subjected young Hell to harsh training, attempting to awaken his dormant monstrous potential to kill Rezok. However, Hell did not manifest the ferocious bloodlust Scinto demanded.

Enraged by what he perceived as a failed experiment, Scinto cast 14-year-old Hell onto the streets as a homeless, broken orphan. In an ironic twist of fate, the homeless child created to kill Rezok was found by Rezok himself, who took Hell in, nurtured him, and adopted him into his own family.

Scinto's Current Agenda

Having failed to control Hell 40 years ago, Black Dagger Scinto is now focused entirely on BSG. By staying close as a trusted physician in the vanguard, he bides his time until BSG unlocks 100% of Redago, at which point Scinto intends to execute his harvest.
