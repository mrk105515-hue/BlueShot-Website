Official Character Dossier

Berry

Fearless Gambler • Extreme Speed • Wielder of Ogre

"He is insane. He fought the Curse God just for fun, even though he knew he could die. He has no fear, and he loves to gamble."

Personality & Overview

Berry is insane. He has no fear and loves to gamble. That is exactly why his life is in danger now.

He is the adopted son of Zigo Degan and his wife Koyo, and the adopted brother of Suma.

The Curse God Battle & Life-or-Death Crisis

Berry fought the 7,999-year-old Curse God just for fun, even though he knew he could die. After fighting the Curse God, he is now suffering through a life-and-death crisis.

Abilities & Iconic Sword

His abilities and weapon include:

  • Extreme Speed: Unmatched swiftness in combat.
  • Ogre: The world's most powerful sword.
