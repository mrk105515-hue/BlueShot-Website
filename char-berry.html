Berry

The insanely fearless gambler who fought the Curse God for fun, wields the world's most powerful sword 'Ogre', and moves with extreme lightning speed.

"I fought the Curse God just for fun knowing I could die. I love the gamble."

Fearless Nature & The Curse God Duel

Berry is completely insane and knows no fear. He loves to gamble with everything, including his very existence.

When the Curse God was awakened in Episode 24, Berry threw himself into battle against the 7,999-year-old deity purely for the thrill of the gamble, even though he fully understood it could cost him his life. Because of that reckless duel, Berry is currently suffering through a critical life-and-death crisis.

Weapon & Combat Mastery

  • Ogre: The world's most powerful sword, capable of cleaving through ancient supernatural defenses and dimensional barriers.
  • Extreme Speed: Berry moves with blinding velocity, outpacing ordinary human perception and striking before opponents can react.
