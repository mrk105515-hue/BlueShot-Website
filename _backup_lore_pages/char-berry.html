Berry Cade (Ogre Blade)

The adopted son of Emperor Jiggo Degan and the fearless frontline juggernaut who wields the colossal, world-cleaving greatsword known as 'The Ogre'.

"Berry is the wielder of the strongest sword, the Ogre."

Backstory & Imperial Adoption

In the lore of Danger X Zone, few warriors command as much immediate physical awe on the battlefield as Berry Cade. Orphaned during the border conflicts preceding the current era, Berry was discovered by Emperor Jiggo Degan surviving alone against monstrous subterranean beasts through raw tenacity and unyielding willpower.

Recognizing his indomitable warrior spirit, Emperor Jiggo adopted Berry into the royal imperial family, raising him alongside his elder daughter Suma. Under the Emperor's tutelage, Berry was bestowed the imperial family's most demanding physical relic: the monumental two-handed broadsword designated The Ogre.

The Ogre Greatsword & Vanguard Combat Style

Advertisement

Weighing several hundred kilograms and imbued with primordial earth-core enchantments, The Ogre is considered the single most physically devastating heavy sword in the Eastern Empire. Most mortal soldiers cannot even lift the weapon from its scabbard, yet Berry wields it with terrifying fluidity:

  • Kinetic Momentum Cleave: Berry utilizes rotational centrifugal force to swing the Ogre in wide sweeping arcs, cleaving through heavily armored shock troops and enemy siege constructs alike.
  • Ogre Divide (Ultimate Move): Channels his entire internal physical aura into the dense obsidian edge of the sword, executing a downward overhead smash that fractures the earth and creates a linear fissure of shockwave destruction.
  • Heavy Vanguard Stance: Berry plants the broad flat of the Ogre into the terrain, using it as an unbreakable mobile shield that withstands artillery and high-tier energy beams.
Physical Resilience & 94% Attack Power

With an attack power stat of 94%, Berry Cade is the alliance's primary frontline hammer. While Zalta relies on curse explosions and BSG on celestial flame, Berry provides steadfast, unwavering physical dominance that holds the vanguard line against any assault.

Key Battles & Heroic Feats

  • The Canyon Ambush: Defended a narrow mountain pass against a legion of 500 DXZ cyborg assassins, holding the chokepoint single-handedly until Suma deployed tactical artillery.
  • Duel Against the Iron Titan: Brought down the syndicate's colossal obsidian mech by severing its central hydraulic core with a single Ogre Divide strike.
  • The Relief of the Eastern Citadel: Led the frontline charge alongside BSG and Zalta, breaking the outer siege ring established by Hell's vanguard forces.

Imperial Bonds & Brotherhood

  • Suma (Adoptive Sister): Berry looks up to his older sister with deep devotion. While he has the physical might, he defers completely to Suma's strategic genius on the battlefield.
  • Emperor Jiggo Degan (Father): Berry views Jiggo not only as his sovereign Emperor, but as the savior who gave his life purpose and honor.
  • BSG & Zalta: Berry admires BSG's discipline and Zalta's sheer beast intensity, often competing with Zalta to see who can eliminate more enemy officers during major campaigns.
