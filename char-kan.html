Kan

The stoic one-eyed warrior who never smiles, whose signature arsenal consists strictly of Blue Pens for instant teleportation and Red Pens for massive explosives.

"Blue pens for teleportation. Red pens for explosives. That is all I need to dismantle an army."

Personality & Appearance

Kan is a calm, deadly, and enigmatic combatant. He never smiles and lost one of his eyes in a brutal past conflict long ago. Despite his quiet demeanour, his combat efficiency is revered across the Eastern continent.

Signature Weapons: The Only Two Pens

Kan does not carry conventional broadswords or shields. His iconic weapons are ordinary-looking writing pens imbued with spatial alchemy:

  • Blue Pens = Teleportation: By deploying or flicking a blue pen, Kan can instantly shift spatial coordinates, teleporting himself, allies, or targets across the battlefield.
  • Red Pens = Explosives: Red pens act as concentrated thermobaric detonators that trigger massive, high-impact explosions on impact.

These are the only two types of pens Kan has ever used throughout his entire combat career.

Alliances & The Three Shades

Kan is an old friend and respected comrade of the Three Shades—an elite mercenary trio consisting of Shane (The Gunman), Gain (Monster Strength), and Fin (The multi-talented fighting guitarist).

Together with BSG, Suma, Berry, and the Three Shades, Kan is at the frontlines trying to thwart Hell's catastrophic plan against Flama Nation.
