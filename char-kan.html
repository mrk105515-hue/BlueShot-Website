Kan (Teleporting Bomber)

The unconventional master assassin who manipulates space-time coordinates using specialized writing implements, deploying high-yield explosives with pinpoint accuracy.

"A mysterious individual with the ability to teleport using pens and create explosives."

The Enigmatic Assassin

In a cosmos dominated by swordsmen, emperors, and cosmic hosts, Kan stands apart as an unpredictable, highly specialized combat artisan. Emerging from the shadowed mercenary underworld of the neutral territories, Kan originally operated as a lone-wolf demolitionist taking on high-risk contracts against corrupt syndicate warlords.

Possessing an unconventional genius for spatial physics and alchemy, Kan engineered a revolutionary method of short-range dimensional displacement that completely baffles standard sensory trackers.

The Pen Teleportation Mechanics & Explosive Alchemy

Advertisement

Kan's primary combat methodology revolves around his signature Dimensional Inscription Pens:

  • Spatial Anchor Inscription: By flicking or throwing specialized pens across the battlefield, Kan inscribes temporary quantum coordinate runes in mid-air or into physical surfaces.
  • Instantaneous Displacement: At will, Kan can instantly swap positions with any active inscribed pen, teleporting behind enemy lines or evading fatal strikes with a 95% agility rating.
  • Explosive Chain Reaction (Ultimate Move): Kan embeds miniature volatile alchemical capsules within his teleportation anchors. Upon triggering, he teleports rapidly between multiple markers, detonating an interconnected chain of high-temperature plasma explosions that trap enemies in an inescapable blast matrix.
Tactical Synergy with BSG & Suma

Kan is the ultimate battlefield disruptor. In joint operations, Suma uses her tactical grid to designate priority targets, Eysa detects enemy blind spots, and Kan teleports into the heart of the enemy formation to detonate strategic supply caches or eliminate high-value officers before escaping unharmed.

Weapon Arsenal & Demolition Gear

  • Dimensional Inscription Pens: High-density carbon-alloy pens infused with spatial displacement crystals.
  • Custom Plasma Micro-Charges: Compact, remote-detonated explosives capable of breaching heavy reinforced obsidian shielding.
  • Smoke & Flash Dispersion Pellets: Deployed during mid-teleportation transitions to disorient enemy visual trackers.
  • Concealed Throwing Blades: Backup secondary weapons coated in non-lethal neurotoxins for silent stealth takedowns.

Key Allegiances & Comrades

  • BSG: Kan holds immense respect for BSG's resilience, frequently providing extraction support and explosive diversions to assist BSG against massive syndicate swarms.
  • Suma: Respects Suma's military genius and follows her tactical coordinates with absolute precision.
  • Black Dagger (Scinto): Kan was among the first to recognize the explosive traps laid out by Black Dagger's cybernetic clones, clashing fiercely with syndicate operatives across the borderlands.
