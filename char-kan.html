Official Character Dossier

Kan

The One-Eyed Pen Master • BSG Vanguard

"He never smiles. He lost one eye long ago. His iconic weapons? Pens. Yeah, pens."

Personality & Overview

Kan is a stoic member of BSG's team currently fighting to stop Emperor Hell's plan to completely devastate Flama Nation.

He never smiles, and he lost one eye long ago in his past battles.

Iconic Weapons: The Two Pens

His iconic weapons are pens. With them, he can teleport and create massive explosions. These are the only two types of pens he has ever used:

  • Blue Pens: Teleportation.
  • Red Pens: Explosives.

Comrades: The Three Shades

Kan is old friends with the Three Shades (Shane the Gunman, Gain with monster-like strength, and Fin the fighting guitarist), who have now joined as allies of his team to help stop Hell's war.
