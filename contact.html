Studio Support & Community Help

Contact & Community FAQ

Have questions about Danger X Zone merchandise, episode releases, or business partnerships? Explore our comprehensive answers below or send us a direct message.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

1. What material are the Danger X Zone T-shirts made of?

All official BlueShot T-shirts are constructed from premium 240 GSM 100% Super-Combed French Terry Cotton. They feature bio-washed pre-shrunk fabric, reinforced double-stitched hems, and vibrant high-density screen printing designed to withstand dozens of wash cycles without fading.

2. How long does shipping and delivery take?

Orders are typically processed, packed, and dispatched within 24–48 business hours. Delivery across major metro cities takes 3–5 business days, and 5–7 business days for rest of India. You will receive an SMS and email containing your live tracking number upon dispatch.

3. How does the Customer Referral Program work?

When you log into your Faction Hub account, you receive a unique referral link (e.g. ?ref=YOURNAME-1234). When 5 friends make a purchase using your link, you automatically unlock a 100% Free T-Shirt Reward of your choice delivered directly to your doorstep.

4. Where can I watch Danger X Zone Season 3?

All official episodes, trailers, and side stories are streamed completely free in full HD on our official YouTube channel: @BlueShot77.

5. What is your return and exchange policy?

We offer a hassle-free 7-Day Replacement Policy for any items that arrive with manufacturing defects, incorrect sizing, or shipping damage. Please review our Refund Policy for step-by-step instructions.

6. What is the 1v1 Battle Arena mini-game?

The DXZ Battle Arena (game.html) is our interactive browser game where you can choose heroes like BSG, Zalta, or Hell to duel in fast-paced kinetic combat and climb the global Season 1 leaderboard!

7. Can I create fan art or YouTube reaction videos?

Yes! We actively encourage and celebrate community creativity. Fan art, theory videos, and review commentaries that follow fair-use principles are welcome. Please tag @blueshot_x so we can feature your work!

8. How do I contact the studio for business partnerships?

For commercial sponsorships, merchandise collaborations, or licensing opportunities, please email our production desk directly at business@blueshotwiki.com or reach out via our Discord management channel.
